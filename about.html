The Philosophy of Time

Why we need a Natural Calendar

The Problem

The Gregorian calendar we use today is an artificial construct, disconnected from the natural rhythms of the Earth and Cosmos. It uses irregular months (30, 31, 28 days) that have no relation to nature, forcing our biology into a mechanized, jagged rhythm.

We believe that Time is Art, not just money. By realigning our measurement of time with natural cycles, we can realign our consciousness with the planet.

The current calendar system fragments our experience of time, creating artificial deadlines and rhythms that conflict with our biological and psychological needs. It treats time as a commodity to be managed rather than a natural flow to be experienced.

The Solution: 13 Moons, 28 Days

Nature loves the number 28. It is the average biological rhythm of the human menstrual cycle and closely tracks the lunar cycle. The Natural Calendar is built on this harmony:

365 Days = 1 Day + (13 Months × 28 Days)

1. The Structure

Every year is divided into exactly 13 Months (Vernis, Germen, Flora, etc.). Every month is exactly 28 Days long. This creates a perfect, consistent rhythm where every month feels the same, stabilizing our mental and physical energy.

This mathematical perfection creates a predictable, harmonious structure that aligns with natural cycles. Unlike the chaotic Gregorian calendar, every month is identical in length, making planning and scheduling intuitive and stress-free.

2. Aeterna: The Day Out of Time

To align the 13x28 structure (364 days) with the solar year (365 days), we have Aeterna.

Aeterna acts as Day 0 of the year. It falls on the Vernal Equinox (March 20th). It belongs to no month and no week. It is a day of pause, recalibration, and pure existence before the cycle begins anew.

This sacred day represents a moment outside the normal flow of time—a chance to reflect, reset, and prepare for the year ahead. It's a day for celebration, meditation, and connection with the natural world.

The 4-Day Rhythm

The standard 7-day week is a relentless grind. We propose a more humane rhythm tailored to peak performance and rest.

  • 3 Days of Creation (Work): Focused, high-intensity contribution.
  • 1 Day of Restoration (Rest): Complete disconnect to recharge.

This creates a cycle of 4 Days. In a 28-day month, there are exactly 7 of these cycles. This rhythm prevents burnout and aligns with periods of intense focus followed by necessary recovery.

Research in chronobiology suggests that our natural attention and energy cycles operate in shorter bursts than the traditional week. The 4-day cycle respects our biological need for regular rest while maintaining productivity and creativity.

The 13 Months: Meanings and Cycles

Each month in the Natural Calendar is named to reflect the natural cycles and energies of that period. These names connect us to the rhythms of the Earth and help us understand the flow of the year.

1. Vernis

Spring awakening, renewal, new beginnings. The first month marks the vernal equinox and the start of the natural year.

2. Germen

Germination, growth, potential. Seeds sprout and life begins to emerge from the earth.

3. Flora

Flowering, blooming, beauty. Nature reaches its peak of color and fragrance.

4. Sol

Sun, peak summer, radiance. The longest days and strongest solar energy.

5. Aestus

Heat, intensity, passion. The warmth of summer reaches its zenith.

6. Serere

Sowing, planting, intention. Late summer planting for future harvests.

7. Fructus

Fruit, abundance, harvest. The first fruits of the season ripen.

8. Messis

Harvest, reaping, gathering. The main harvest season brings abundance.

9. Autumnus

Autumn, transition, change. Leaves turn and nature prepares for rest.

10. Bruma

Winter solstice, depth, reflection. The shortest day marks the turning point.

11. Niveus

Snow, purity, stillness. Winter's quiet beauty covers the land.

12. Glacies

Ice, preservation, waiting. The deepest cold before the thaw.

13. Renova

Renewal, rebirth, preparation. The final month before the cycle begins again.

Lunar Alignment

While the calendar provides a stable Solar structure (13 fixed months), we acknowledge that the Moon has its own fluid rhythm (29.5 days). We do not force the Moon to fit the Month.

Instead, we overlay the Moon Phase on every single day. This allows you to see the "Lunar Drift"—how the fluid lunar energy moves through the stable solar framework.

  • New Moon: Inward energy, intention setting, new beginnings.
  • Waxing Crescent: Building momentum, taking action.
  • First Quarter: Decision-making, commitment.
  • Waxing Gibbous: Refinement, adjustment, preparation.
  • Full Moon: Outward energy, realization, peak emotion, completion.
  • Waning Gibbous: Gratitude, sharing, teaching.
  • Last Quarter: Release, forgiveness, letting go.
  • Waning Crescent: Surrender, rest, reflection.

By tracking both solar and lunar cycles simultaneously, we gain a deeper understanding of natural rhythms and can align our activities with the most appropriate energies for each phase.

Benefits of the Natural Calendar

  • Consistency: Every month is exactly 28 days, eliminating confusion and making planning predictable.
  • Biological Alignment: Matches natural human cycles and rhythms.
  • Reduced Stress: Predictable structure reduces anxiety about deadlines and scheduling.
  • Natural Awareness: Connects us to seasonal changes and lunar cycles.
  • Work-Life Balance: The 4-day rhythm promotes sustainable productivity with regular rest.
  • Mathematical Elegance: Simple, beautiful structure that makes sense.
